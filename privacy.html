Cadenzia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short Cadenzia processes your health data exclusively on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no account, no server, no tracking and no advertising. We receive none of your data.

Controller Dr. Nathan Seefelder (sole proprietorship), Portenstraße 8, 81379 Munich, Germany. Email: nathan.seefelder@googlemail.com

What data Cadenzia reads With your permission, Cadenzia reads from Apple Health: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workout heart rate, sleep analysis, wrist temperature, menstrual data, contraception, pregnancy and lactation, and workouts. From this, Cadenzia calculates your recovery score locally. You decide in the Health settings which data Cadenzia may read, and you can change this at any time.

What data Cadenzia writes Your optional morning check-in (“How do you feel?”) as a daily mood in Apple Health. And only if you use “My period started”: the date of your period as cycle tracking data in Apple Health, without flow or symptoms. Nothing else.

Storage and transfer Calculated values, settings and a short activity log (without health values) are stored only on your devices. Between iPhone and Watch, only settings such as the selected mode are synced via Apple’s device connection. Health data never leaves your devices. The activity log is shared only if you send it yourself via “Share diagnostics”.

Purchases The purchase of Cadenzia Pro is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data and no personal data about you.

No analytics, no sharing Cadenzia contains no third-party analytics or advertising software and shares no data. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Your rights Since we receive no personal data, all data is in your hands: you can revoke access in Apple Health and remove all values stored by Cadenzia by deleting the app. For questions, contact us at the email address above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Not a medical device Cadenzia is a wellness app for training and recovery. It makes no diagnoses, does not assess symptoms and gives no treatment recommendations.
